The entry-visa fee has no authorised amount

The amount is typed by an agent and saved unconditionally — no price list, no cap, no approval routing. ENTRY_VISA_COST_INSIDE_UAE = 900 and ENTRY_VISA_COST_OUTSIDE_UAE = 400 are dead code: getEntryVisaCost() has zero callers and is @JsonIgnore. The auto-costing service that once used them was commented out in 2018. The only server-side code touching the amount is the July-2025 reclassification, which rewrites ENTRY_VSIA to ENTRY_VISA_LESS_THAN_1000 at or below a threshold of 1000 — a relabel, not a control.

Every unit-price test downstream is therefore empirical rather than authoritative. It anchors on the measured modes — AED 1,022.50 and 372.50, which carry 89% and 88% of their bands. The refund side is now proven: the government keeps a flat AED 283.00, confirmed against 1,287 refunds whose variances sum to zero. Accounting’s accounting’s getDefaultEntryVisaExpenses() fallback of 1073 / 403 is a stale comparator. This affects every charge in the population — which is exactly why it carries no row verdict. A finding that reds every row would make green unreachable and turn gross exposure into the entire ledger. It changes how M6 is read, and the colour of nothing.

Verdicts are seven values: Green · Red · Candidate · Not applicable · Void · Blocked · Reported. One red outweighs any number of greens, and one blocked does too. Not applicable exists so that “the test never fired” cannot collapse into “the charge passed”.
Rejection is read from both sources and unioned: the revision history alone misses 196 of 868 rejected requests, the live column alone misses 360. Live-only cases are rejected but undated, so every ordering and ageing test on them is blocked, not green.
Export and drill-down carry ids, counts and amounts only. Staff names are blocklisted from every surface, including DESCRIPTION, which concatenates a passport number.
