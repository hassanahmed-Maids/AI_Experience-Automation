The entry-visa fee has no authorised amount

The amount is typed by an agent and saved unconditionally — no price list, no cap, no approval routing. ENTRY_VISA_COST_INSIDE_UAE = 900 and ENTRY_VISA_COST_OUTSIDE_UAE = 400 are dead code: getEntryVisaCost() has zero callers and is @JsonIgnore. The auto-costing service that once used them was commented out in 2018. The only server-side code touching the amount is the July-2025 reclassification, which rewrites ENTRY_VSIA to ENTRY_VISA_LESS_THAN_1000 at or below a threshold of 1000 — a relabel, not a control.

Every unit-price test downstream is therefore empirical rather than authoritative, anchored on accounting’s getDefaultEntryVisaExpenses() fallback of 1073 / 403. That is a default, not a tariff. This affects every charge in the population.

Verdicts are six values: Green · Red · Candidate · Void · Blocked · Reported. One red outweighs any number of greens, and one blocked does too.
Rejection is read only from INITIAL_VISA_REQUESTS_HISTORY revisions, never from the live column — it is overwritten on re-application and loses exactly the re-applied cases.
Export is at row grain: ids, counts and amounts only. No names, no contact details, no salaries.
