Visa Expense Audit Console Design mockup

Seven Police & Control audits over one visa-expense chain, on one clock. Figures are the measurements in each spec, dated 3–14 Sep 2026; table rows are the specs' own worked examples.

Design mockup for review — not a live report. Maids appear as Maid #id only; no names, note text or descriptions are shown or exported anywhere in this design.
